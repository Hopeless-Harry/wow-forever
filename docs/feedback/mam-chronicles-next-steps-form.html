My reading of your note

The game already counts a lot of what we want

Every character has a built-in Statistics tab in the Achievements window (deaths, quests completed, creatures killed, dungeons entered, distance travelled and so on). The client keeps these numbers for the character's whole life, not just since we installed. An addon reads them with GetStatisticsCategoryList, GetCategoryNumAchievements, GetAchievementInfo and GetStatistic.

Why it is useful for us: today the Chronicle only knows what happened after install, and it only records the achievement earned event. Reading statistics would give every character a real starting baseline on day one, let monthly summaries show "+14 deaths this month" as a difference between snapshots, and feed the awards.

What I have not done: I have not yet run these calls in your game, so I have not checked which statistics exist on your client or what they return. I would discover them by category and name at runtime instead of hard-coding numbers, since IDs differ between clients. Forever is unverified.
